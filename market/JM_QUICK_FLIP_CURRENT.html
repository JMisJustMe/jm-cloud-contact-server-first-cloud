What is doing the work underneath

Loading your JM Coding Estate…

Your JM Coding Estate is doing the checking underneath: JMLogic decides whether it is worth a look or a hold, Route-Code runs the route, ContactCode proves the Kraken ↔ Coinbase contact, and TraceBox keeps the receipt.

What this does NOT do

It does not buy or sell anything. It is a live checker using public prices and your chosen costs. A positive result means “worth another look”, not “put money in”. Real fills, account fees, timing, transfers and tax can change the outcome.
